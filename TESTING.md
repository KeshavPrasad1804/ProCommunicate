# Testing ProCommunicate on your iPhone 15

Everything is built and installed. Work through this list once; note anything odd (a screenshot helps).
Free Apple ID: the install expires 7 days after the last install from Xcode. Plug in and ask for a reinstall when it stops opening.

## 0. Setup (Wi-Fi, charger)
- [ ] First launch: the logo animation, then the **welcome** pages (About you, daily goal, AI tutor). Turn the AI tutor on.
- [ ] **Download both models** on the last welcome page or in Settings ⚙️: AI tutor (3.11 GB) and speech (≈217 MB).
  - [ ] Try **Pause**, **Resume**, and leaving the app during the big download (it should continue).
  - [ ] Both show **Ready · works offline**.
- [ ] Settings → **About you**: fill in name, role, project, what you're preparing for.

## 1. AI test lab (Settings → AI test lab): send me the results
- [ ] **1. Load the model**: load time, memory, "left before iOS closes the app".
- [ ] **2. Role-play test**: tokens/s and first-token time, and do the replies make sense?
- [ ] **3. Answer-check test**, then **Check this sentence** with a few of your own.
- [ ] Tap the share icon next to **Results** and send them.
- If the app **closes during Load**, iOS ran out of memory: tell me.

## 2. Lessons and review
- [ ] Learn → any unit → **Start lesson**. All 22 units have lessons now. Try every exercise type, get one wrong on purpose (it should come back at the end).
- [ ] The next day: Review → say each phrase aloud, rate Again/Hard/Good/Easy.

## 3. Voice: the most important tests
- [ ] **Stand-up drill** (Today): record a real update. Does the transcript match **exactly** what you said, including mistakes like "I am having…"? (If Whisper quietly fixes your grammar, tell me.) Do you get one useful fix?
- [ ] **Quick role-play** (Practice → Status update to a UK client), with earphones and without:
  - [ ] Does Emma's voice come through, and does she reply within about **2–3 seconds** after you stop?
  - [ ] Does she cut in while you're still thinking? (Settings → Calls → *Wait before the AI replies*; or tap **I'm done**.)
  - [ ] Start talking while she speaks: does she stop (interrupt)?
  - [ ] Without earphones: does she ever "hear herself" and reply to her own voice?
- [ ] End the call → **report**: your numbers, top fixes (play *What I said*, tap *தமிழில்*), *Say it better*, *Try this answer again*.

## 4. Full calls (10–15 minutes)
- [ ] Practice → **Weekly status call with a UK client**: the whole call. Does the app stay open and responsive to the end? Does the phone get hot? Battery used?
- [ ] Practice → **HR screening interview** (paste a real job description in the optional box).

## 5. The rest
- [ ] Practice → **I have a meeting tomorrow**: describe a real meeting in Tanglish → cheat sheet → **Rehearse this call**.
- [ ] Practice → **Ask in Tamil**: e.g. "manager kitta naalaikku leave kekkanum".
- [ ] Settings → **Speaking check**.
- [ ] Progress: weekly report, fluency over calls, My Mistakes, call history; Today's fix appears after a call.
- [ ] Settings → **Daily reminder** at your time.

## 6. Tamil
- [ ] Read TAMIL_REVIEW.md and tell me any Tamil that sounds wrong or unnatural.

## Adaptive plan (new)

- [ ] **Today → each step shows a 💡 reason.** Today's lesson should follow your focus (Settings → Your plan → Focus).
- [ ] **Repeated mistake:** say "I will revert back" in two or three calls. Within a day or two, Today's lesson should become *Getting back to people* ("Fixes a mistake you keep making…").
- [ ] **Learn tab:** units you've done show a mastery bar ("Mastered", or "needs a refresh" once it fades).
- [ ] **Meeting prep with a date:** set it to tomorrow, make the cheat sheet, then go back to Today. The call step should be "Rehearse your real meeting tomorrow".
- [ ] **AI-written content:** with the AI tutor on, stay on Today for ~10 s once a day. The phrase card says "for your job", and the weekly card says "Written for your job". **Check the Tamil.**
- [ ] **Goal reading:** in About you, set "Preparing for" to something like "Zoho interview next Friday". Focus (Auto) should switch to job interviews, and a dated event should appear.
- [ ] **Weekly level chip** (Gentle / Standard / Challenging) rises as your mistakes per answer fall.
- [ ] **Settings → About → Storage:** shows sizes, and "Clean up now" works.

## Hindi (new)
- [ ] **Settings → Language → Hindi.** Lessons, Review cards, mistake notes, Ask in Hindi and the phrase of the day all switch to Hindi, and your progress stays.
- [ ] **A Hindi call report:** tap "हिंदी में" on a fix. The AI's explanation should be in Devanagari, in everyday Hindi.
- [ ] **Ask in Hindi with Hinglish**, e.g. "manager se kal ki chhutti maangni hai". The answer gives English sentences you can save.
- [ ] **Switch back to Tamil.** Everything returns to Tamil.
