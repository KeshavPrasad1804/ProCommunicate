# ProCommunicate: how it works and how it helps you learn

For a Tamil speaker who wants to **speak** confident spoken, professional and business English.

## Your focus (set 2026-11-09)
1. **Speaking at work in business and client meetings**: the biggest area
2. **Job interviews**: the second priority

Everything below is ordered around these two. General spoken English and email writing support them but come later.

---

## 1. How you will learn (the method behind every feature)

| Principle | What it means in the app |
|---|---|
| **Speak every day** | Speaking minutes are the main goal, not lessons completed. Every session ends with you talking |
| **Learn phrases, not rules** | You learn ready-to-use phrases (*"Could you please…"*, *"I'll get back to you by…"*), with a short grammar note only when needed |
| **Real situations from your work** | Interviews, manager talks, client calls, meetings, emails, office small talk |
| **Few corrections, repeated until fixed** | After each practice you get your **top 1–3 fixes**, not 20 red marks. Each one comes back until you stop making it |
| **Your own mistakes become your lessons** | Everything you get wrong goes into *My Mistakes* and turns into practice made from your own sentences |
| **Review at the right time** | Phrases and fixes come back just before you would forget them (spaced repetition) |
| **Tamil is a bridge, not a crutch** | Explanations and help in Tamil at the start; the app slowly reduces Tamil as your level rises (you can change it) |
| **Safe place to practise** | Private, offline, no judgement. Retry any answer as many times as you like |

---

## 2. Your daily session (10, 20 or 30 minutes, you choose)

A 20-minute day:

1. **Stand-up drill (2 min):** speak your real daily update out loud, the way you would in your team's stand-up: *yesterday, today, blockers*. The app times it and gives one fix. Two minutes of real work English, every day
2. **Warm-up review (3 min):** say the phrases and fixes that are due today, out loud
3. **Today's lesson (6 min):** 3–5 phrases for one meeting or interview situation → listen → repeat → use them in your own sentence
4. **Speak with the AI (6 min):** a short client-call or interview role-play that uses today's phrases
5. **Today's fix (3 min):** your #1 mistake from today, with a Tamil explanation, said correctly 3 times

**Weekly, alternating:**
- **Week A:** a full **client meeting simulation** (a status call with a UK client and your project manager)
- **Week B:** a full **mock interview** for your role

Each ends with a report, followed by your **weekly report**.

## 3. Features

### A. Getting started
1. **Goal setup:** choose what you are preparing for (job interview, current job, client calls, general speaking), your role (for example, Java developer, sales, support) and your daily time
2. **Speaking placement test (3 min):** introduce yourself, describe your job, answer 2 questions. The app sets your starting level for speaking, grammar, vocabulary and fluency
3. **Personal plan:** a week-by-week path built from your goal and level

### B. Learn
4. **Phrase lessons:** short units, ordered by your focus (full outline in section 4):
   - **Track 1: Meetings and client calls** (first)
   - **Track 2: Job interviews** (second)
   - Track 3: Everyday work English (small talk, your manager, leave, apologies), mixed in as support
   - Track 4: Emails and messages (later)
5. **Casual vs professional:** see the same idea at both levels (*"send it asap"* → *"Could you please send it by end of day?"*)
6. **Common Tamil-speaker mistakes:** a track of its own (*Myself…*, *I am having…*, *revert back*, *do the needful*, *today itself*, *discuss about*, *since 2019 I am working*, *cousin brother*, *prepone*, *What is your good name?*), each with a Tamil explanation and the natural alternative
7. **Listening practice:** short workplace dialogues (meetings, calls) in Indian, British and American English voices, at normal or slower speed, with questions

### C. Speak

**Call mode: how the meeting simulation, mock interview and role-plays work**

Real interviews and client meetings happen on phone, Teams or Zoom calls, so practice happens the same way:
- **A call screen, not a chat screen.** The AI interviewer or client *speaks*; you *answer out loud*. There's no text to read during the call, just like a real call (you can turn on subtitles while you're starting out)
- **Hands-free turn-taking.** The app hears when you've finished speaking (a short pause) and the other person replies. You can **interrupt** them, as you would on a real call
- **Natural timing.** Short acknowledgements (*"Mm-hmm"*, *"Okay, I see"*) play instantly while the AI prepares its reply, so there are no awkward silences
- **Real call conditions (optional):** **phone-line sound** (narrow, slightly noisy audio, as on a real phone interview), **UK, US or Indian English** voices, and the other person speaking at full speed
- **Earphones or AirPods supported,** with echo cancellation so the AI doesn't hear itself
- **Surprise call (later):** you set a window (for example, *"sometime between 6 and 8 pm"*), and an HR "call" notification arrives. You answer and do a 5-minute screening interview without preparing, like a real recruiter call
- **Everything is recorded** (your voice and the AI's), with a transcript, so you can listen back (see *Recordings* below)

8. **Listen and repeat (shadowing):** hear a sentence, say it, see what you said compared with the original, and try again
9. **AI role-plays:** realistic conversations: HR interviewer, manager, UK client, colleague, customer. You speak; the AI replies like a real person
   - **"How do I say this?" button:** ask in Tamil or Tanglish mid-conversation (*"deadline extend panna eppadi kekkanum?"*) and get the English phrase
   - **"Say it better" after each reply:** see a more natural version of what you just said, then say it again
   - **Practice-only mode:** no corrections at all, just talking, for building confidence
10. **Client meeting simulation (full):** a 15–20 minute call where the AI plays the **UK client** and sometimes your **project manager**. It covers the opening small talk, your status update, a tough question (*"Why is this delayed again?"*), agreeing next steps and closing the call. Scenarios include status calls, requirement discussions, escalations, demo walkthroughs and sprint reviews. Ends with a **meeting report**: top 3 fixes quoted from what you said, how clearly you gave your update, whether you used meeting phrases (clarifying, disagreeing politely, summarising), fluency numbers, and **replay any moment**
11. **Mock interview (full):** 15–25 questions for your role, with follow-ups on what you said. Optionally paste the job description and your resume so the questions match. Ends with an **interview report**:
    - your top 3 habits to fix, quoted from your own answers
    - your best answer, and a better version of your weakest answer
    - fluency numbers (speaking speed, fillers, long pauses)
    - **retry any question** and compare
12. **Real-life prep ("I have a meeting tomorrow"):** describe your real meeting or interview in English, Tamil or Tanglish (*"naalaikku client call la delay pathi sollanum"*). The app gives you a **cheat sheet** of key phrases and a **5-minute rehearsal** of that exact conversation. This moves up because it helps on real workdays
13. **Stand-up drill:** your daily *yesterday / today / blockers* update, spoken and timed, with one fix each day
14. **Pronunciation practice:** sounds Tamil speakers often find hard (*v / w*, *z / j*, *th*, *-ed* endings, word stress such as *deVElopment*, *comPAny*), with listen-and-repeat pairs. Basic check: did the app hear the word you meant?

### D. Write
15. **Email and message coach:** write your own draft → the app improves it and explains each change in Tamil. Templates for leave, delays, follow-ups, apologies, deadline extensions, meeting requests
16. **Ask in Tamil:** type in Tamil script or Tanglish, get 1–2 professional English sentences ready to use, and save them to your phrasebook

### E. Recordings
17. **Session recordings:** every call, role-play, stand-up drill and answer is saved as audio with a transcript, kept in sync. Tap any sentence in the transcript to hear yourself say it
18. **Listen back with fixes:** in each report, every fix links to the exact moment you said it. Hear the original, then hear the correct version, then record yourself again
19. **Answer comparisons:** record the same answer (for example, *"Tell me about yourself"* or your status update) over weeks and play the versions side by side, with speaking speed, fillers and pauses for each
20. **Analyse my recording:** import an audio file of **yourself** speaking: a practice run you recorded elsewhere, or your part of a real meeting **when everyone has agreed to it** (for example, a meeting recording your company shares). The app transcribes it and gives you the same report. *(iPhone apps cannot record phone calls, and recording other people needs their consent. The first version handles recordings of your own voice; separating several speakers comes later, if it works well on the phone)*
21. **Storage control:** recordings stay on the phone only (excluded from cloud backup unless you choose otherwise), and you can star the ones to keep. Older ones are cleaned up automatically after a period you choose (about 1 MB per minute of audio)

### F. Remember and improve
22. **My Mistakes notebook:** every mistake from every session, grouped by pattern (for example, *tense with "since"*), how often you make it, and whether you are improving
23. **Personal practice drills:** generated from your own mistakes (fix the sentence, say it correctly, use it in a new sentence)
24. **Review (spaced repetition):** phrases and fixes due today, practised by **speaking**, not just tapping
25. **My Phrasebook:** phrases you saved from lessons, role-plays and *Ask in Tamil*

### G. Progress and motivation
26. **Skill levels:** speaking, fluency, grammar, vocabulary and professional tone, each tracked separately
27. **Fluency tracker:** words per minute, filler words (*umm*, *actually*, *basically*), long pauses, longest answer, all over time
28. **Weekly report:** for example, *"You spoke for 74 minutes this week. You stopped saying 'Myself…'. Next focus: 'since' with 'have been'."*
29. **Streaks and daily goal**, with gentle reminders at the time you choose
30. **Before and after:** your self-introduction and status update from day 1 next to today's, with the numbers

### H. Trust
31. **Fully private and offline:** your voice, answers and mistakes never leave the phone
32. **The tutor stays on task:** it brings off-topic chats back to practice and doesn't produce harmful content

## 4. Lesson outline for your two focus tracks

Each unit: 3–5 key phrases → listen and repeat → use them in your own sentence → short AI role-play → your fix.

### Track 1: Meetings and client calls
| # | Unit | Example phrases you will own |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Daily stand-up** | *"Yesterday I completed…"*, *"Today I'll be working on…"*, *"I'm blocked on… and need help from…"* |
| 2 | **Opening a call and small talk** | *"Can everyone hear me?"*, *"How was your weekend?"*, *"Shall we get started?"* |
| 3 | **Giving a status update** | *"We're on track for…"*, *"We've completed 80% of…"*, *"The next milestone is…"* |
| 4 | **Explaining a delay or a problem** | *"Unfortunately, we've run into an issue with…"*, *"The new timeline is…"*, *"To avoid this, we'll…"* |
| 5 | **Asking for clarification** | *"Could you clarify what you mean by…?"*, *"Just to confirm, you'd like…?"*, *"Sorry, could you repeat that?"* |
| 6 | **Handling a question you can't answer** | *"Let me check and get back to you by…"* (instead of *revert back*), *"That's a good question; I'll confirm with the team"* |
| 7 | **Agreeing, disagreeing politely, pushing back** | *"I see your point, but…"*, *"I'd suggest a different approach…"*, *"That may be difficult because…"* |
| 8 | **Interrupting and joining in** | *"Sorry to interrupt, but…"*, *"If I could add something…"* |
| 9 | **Negotiating scope and deadlines** | *"If we move the deadline to…, we can include…"*, *"Could we prioritise…?"* |
| 10 | **Demo and presenting your work** | *"Let me share my screen"*, *"As you can see here…"*, *"Any questions so far?"* |
| 11 | **Escalations and difficult clients** | *"I understand your concern"*, *"Here's what we're doing to fix it"* |
| 12 | **Summarising and closing** | *"To summarise…"*, *"The action items are…"*, *"I'll send the minutes after the call"* |
| ★ | **Listening: UK and US clients** | Fast speech, idioms (*"touch base"*, *"ballpark figure"*, *"circle back"*) |

### Track 2: Job interviews
| # | Unit | Example phrases you will own |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Tell me about yourself"** | A 60–90 second answer built from your real background (no *"Myself…"*) |
| 2 | **Current role and responsibilities** | *"In my current role, I'm responsible for…"*, *"I've been working at … since…"* |
| 3 | **Projects using STAR** (Situation, Task, Action, Result) | *"The challenge was…"*, *"I led…"*, *"As a result, response time dropped by…"* |
| 4 | **Technical explanations in simple English** | Explaining your stack and design choices clearly |
| 5 | **Teamwork and conflict** | *"We had a disagreement about…"*, *"I suggested we…"* |
| 6 | **Strengths and weaknesses** | Honest, professional answers that don't sound memorised |
| 7 | **Why are you leaving? / Why this company?** | Positive framing |
| 8 | **Salary, notice period, joining date** | *"My expected CTC is…"*, *"My notice period is 60 days, negotiable"* |
| 9 | **Questions to ask the interviewer** | *"What does success look like in this role?"* |
| 10 | **HR and managerial rounds** | Situational questions, handling *"I don't know"* gracefully |

### Supporting every unit
The **common Tamil-speaker mistakes** that appear in meetings and interviews: *Myself…*, *I am having…*, *I am working here since…*, *revert back*, *do the needful*, *today itself*, *discuss about*, *prepone*, *same page*, *little bit*.
