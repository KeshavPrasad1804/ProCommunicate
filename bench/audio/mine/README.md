# Your voice clips for the listening test

Record each clip in **Voice Memos** on your iPhone. Speak the way you normally would, not the exact words below. Then share each memo, AirDrop it to the Mac, and drop it in this folder named `1.m4a` … `6.m4a`.

1. **Tamil:** ask how to say *"the project will be delayed by one week"* in English.
2. **Tanglish (mixed):** ask how to tell the client you need two more days for testing.
3. **Stuck mid-sentence:** start in English and switch to Tamil when you get stuck, e.g. "So the testing is… enna solradhu… innum mudiyala, naalaikku mudichiduvom".
4. **Tamil:** "I didn't understand the question, please explain it again."
5. **English:** "Myself [your name] and I am having [N] years of experience in [your skill]."
6. **English:** "I will revert back to you by today itself."

Clips 5 and 6 check that a multilingual model still hears your English word for word, mistakes included.
